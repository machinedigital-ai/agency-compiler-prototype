# Agency Compiler review identity

## Style prompt

Owner-facing creative operations in a calm, tactile paper-and-field palette. The review experience is editorial and direct: a work product is the focal object, while its provenance and approval decision stay visible without competing for attention.

## Colors

- `#f4f6f1` - paper background
- `#ffffff` - artifact surface
- `#171917` - primary ink
- `#497228` - owner-action green
- `#d9ff3f` - approval accent

## Typography

- DM Sans for artifact reading and decisions
- DM Mono for provenance and measured data

## Motion

Short transform-and-opacity entrance only. No looping animation, no decorative particle effects, and reduced motion resolves immediately.

## What not to do

- Do not imply an artifact was rendered, published, or delivered.
- Do not use AI-purple gradients, fake metrics, or decorative status markers.
- Do not use dark panels as decoration; preserve the light owner-workspace theme.
